Alchemist's Last Stand

A VR Potion Mixing & Combat Game

Mariam Wriglesworth & Jasmine Thind

About

Project Description

In this game, the player finds themselves in an abandoned chemistry lab. Around the room are various potions. The door is boarded up and various enemies are trying to get in. It's up to the player to launch potions at the enemy to defend themselves.

The player can mix potions to create new ones that have different properties when thrown at an enemy — slow them down, disorient them, or eliminate them instantly.

Gameplay

Potion Effects

Mix the potions in the lab to create different chemical weapons. Each resulting colour has a unique effect on enemies.

🧪
Red Potion
Slows the enemy
🧪
Green Potion
Spins & slows
🧪
Mixed Potion
Instant death

Controls

How to Play

  • A Button Teleport to next hotspot
  • Hand Trigger + Touch Grab a potion
  • Throw + Release Launch potion at enemy
